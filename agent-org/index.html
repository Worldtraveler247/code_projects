    House rules

    Why the chart is shaped this way
    • Narrow rolesEach agent has one job and a short instruction set. A specialist is easier to predict than a generalist.
    • Least privilegeEach agent gets only the tools its job needs. A reviewer that cannot write cannot alter what it reviews.
    • Separation of dutiesThe seat that checks work is separate from the seats that produce it. That seat is still vacant, and the chart says so.
    • A human approvesAgents draft and report. Sending, paying, publishing and deleting wait for a person.
